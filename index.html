YoutubeUploader

The upload tool of the Kids Wonderland YouTube channel.

What the app does

YoutubeUploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Kids Wonderland YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own finished videos to the Kids Wonderland channel through the YouTube Data API v3, and sets each video's title, description, tags, "made for kids" designation and visibility.

The app is not offered to the public and has no other users. It does not read, collect or store any information about viewers.

About Kids Wonderland

Kids Wonderland publishes short, original animated sing-along songs for toddlers. Each song teaches one simple thing, such as counting, colors, opposites, animal sounds or a daily routine like brushing teeth. All videos are designated as made for kids.
