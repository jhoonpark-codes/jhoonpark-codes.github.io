Privacy Policy

YoutubeUploader, the upload tool of the Kids Wonderland YouTube channel. Effective October 19, 2026.

Who uses the app

YoutubeUploader is used only by the owner of the Kids Wonderland YouTube channel, to manage that channel. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, given through Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API v3 to:

  • upload the channel's own videos to the Kids Wonderland channel;
  • set each video's title, description, tags, "made for kids" designation and visibility;
  • read the channel's basic information (its name and ID) to confirm the upload goes to the right channel.

How information is used and stored

  • Information is used only to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel.
  • The sign-in credentials are kept only in the owner's private, access-controlled environment.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.
  • This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Viewers and children

The app does not collect any information from people who watch Kids Wonderland videos, including children. Videos are designated as made for kids on YouTube.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Once access is revoked, the app can no longer reach the channel, and the stored credentials are deleted.
